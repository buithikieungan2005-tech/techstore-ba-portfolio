The Challenge

Business Problem & Solution

The Problem

Business data was fragmented across multiple departments, creating operational inefficiencies:

  • • Difficult inventory control and stock visibility
  • • Challenges in order tracking and fulfillment
  • • Limited business performance visibility
  • • Barriers to data-driven decision making

Our Solution

TechStore centralizes business data and standardizes operational processes:

  • Centralized system architecture
  • Standardized business processes
  • Synchronized data across departments
  • Data-driven business intelligence

Critical Processes

Core Business Workflows

Workflow 01

Promotion Approval Workflow

Process Flow

1 Warehouse Manager creates promotion
2 Submit request to Director
3 Director reviews & approves/rejects
4 Notify stakeholders & activate promotion

Business Value

Controls promotion execution, ensures brand consistency, and aligns promotions with business strategy.

Workflow 02

Inventory Distribution Approval Workflow

Process Flow

1 Store Manager creates distribution request
2 Submit request to Director
3 Director reviews & approves/rejects
4 Send to Warehouse Manager for execution
5 Execute distribution & notify stakeholders

Business Value

Optimizes inventory distribution, prevents stockouts, and ensures efficient resource allocation across stores.

Workflow 03

Order Fulfillment Workflow

Process Flow

1 Customer places order
2 System records order
3 Store Employee & Manager process order
4 Warehouse checks stock & exports order
5 Order delivered & completed

Business Value

Streamlines order processing, ensures timely delivery, and improves customer satisfaction through transparency.

Analytical Foundation

System Thinking & Analysis

Analytical Approach

This case study demonstrates a comprehensive approach to understanding how different stakeholders interact within a retail ecosystem and how business requirements translate into structured system models.

Through systematic analysis of stakeholder roles, business processes, and system interactions, we developed an integrated model that supports business operations, data management, and strategic decision-making.

Key Analysis Areas

  • → Stakeholder Analysis: Understanding roles, responsibilities, and interaction patterns
  • → Process Analysis: Mapping business workflows and identifying optimization opportunities
  • → Functional Analysis: Decomposing requirements into system functions and features
  • → Data Flow Analysis: Modeling information movement and system integration
  • → Interaction Modeling: Defining component communication protocols and sequences
  • → Decision Support: Enabling data-driven business decision-making
